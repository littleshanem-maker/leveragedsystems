FAQ

Common questions

Do I need new software?

No. I work with what you've already got. If something does need adding, I'll tell you why before anything happens.

Is my data safe?

Nothing goes out to a client, subbie or supplier without your sign-off.

What if it doesn't work?

Discovery tells you upfront what's worth doing, and if it isn't worth it, I'll say so. After that it's month to month, so you're never locked in to something that isn't paying its way.

How does pricing work?

The first chat is free. If it's worth digging deeper, Discovery is a paid, fixed-fee piece of work. You'll know the fee before you commit to anything, and what comes after depends on what's worth fixing.

Will AI replace my office staff?

That's not the aim. It takes the repetitive typing and chasing off their plate so they can spend time on the work that needs a person.
