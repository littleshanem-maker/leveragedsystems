Questions

Questions

Do I need new software?

No. We work with what you've already got. If something does need adding, we'll tell you why before anything happens.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays yours. We only ask for the access a job actually needs, everything runs through accounts in your business's name, and you can switch our access off at any time. Nothing goes out without your sign-off.

What if it doesn't work?

Discovery tells you upfront what's worth doing, and if it isn't worth it, we'll say so. After that it's month to month, so you're never locked in to something that isn't paying its way.

How does pricing work?

It depends on your business and what's worth fixing. We'll talk it through on the discovery chat, and you'll know exactly where you stand before you commit to anything.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small Australian construction and trade businesses: builders, landscapers, plumbers, electricians, civil and concrete contractors.
