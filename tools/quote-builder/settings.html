WHAT THIS HARNESS DOES

Turn a site visit into a quote you can review.

This harness powers your landscaping and concreting quote builder. It organises your site evidence, asks the AI for a proposed scope, checks the proposed items and calculates the price. After your review, it produces a branded quote PDF.

  1. Capture the jobYour notes, measurements, selected photos, sketches and transcripts describe each work area.
  2. Prepare a proposalThe AI reads your service catalogue and proposes scope, line items and questions about missing information.
  3. Check and priceThe harness checks service references and quantities. You review the scope, quantities and rates; code calculates exact prices and GST.
  4. Create the quoteThe PDF service recalculates the totals and marks incomplete quotes as drafts. You decide when to share the document.

What these settings change: the models, writing preferences, context and limits used during AI processing. The quote workflow and required checks are defined in the software. Manual scope, pricing and PDF export remain available while the AI connection is being set up.
